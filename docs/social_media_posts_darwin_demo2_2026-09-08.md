## LinkedIn (3,000 char limit)

**Recommended images:** Step3.png (lead — shows ortholog comparison), Step2.png, Final.png

Decades of Arabidopsis research. Six crop genomes. One question:

Can we get discoveries out of model organisms and into crops faster?

Darwin's "Model Gene to Crop Target" workflow was built to answer that.

Here's a real example. DREB2A is one of the most studied drought-tolerance transcription factors in Arabidopsis. It activates LTI78, COR15A, LEA14, and dozens of other stress-response genes. The regulatory circuit is well-characterized — backed by ChIP-seq, DAP-seq, and genetic data.

Now you want to find the equivalent gene in a crop.

Darwin finds orthologs in petunia, tomato, rice, potato, and pepper. But they're not equal — and this is the critical part. Darwin shows you exactly how much evidence exists for each one:

In petunia:
- Best ortholog: 1,599 measured edges, 549 conserved targets
- Second: 10 measured edges
- Third: 2 measured edges

In tomato:
- Best ortholog: 467 measured edges, 477 conserved targets
- Second: 3 measured edges
- Third: 0 measured edges — entirely inferred

The choice is obvious — but only because Darwin shows you the evidence. The petunia ortholog has 1,599 experimentally validated regulatory relationships from PlantRegMap and shares 549 conserved targets with Arabidopsis DREB2A. The weakest tomato ortholog has zero measured edges — every relationship is predicted.

Pick the strongest candidate, and Darwin designs a 250nt dsRNA molecule with 100% specificity — no off-targets in the entire genome. It even predicts the downstream cascade: silencing this gene would affect over 17,000 downstream genes.

This is the difference between "we found an ortholog" and "we found an ortholog we can actually work with."

Here's a real example of why evidence quality matters in translational plant science.

DREB2A is a well-studied drought tolerance gene in Arabidopsis. Darwin finds orthologs in petunia, tomato, rice, and more — but they're not equal.

The best petunia ortholog has 1,599 experimentally validated edges and 549 conserved targets. The weakest tomato ortholog has zero measured edges — entirely inferred from other species.

Darwin shows you this difference immediately. Three numbers per ortholog: measured edges, predicted edges, conserved targets. You pick the one with the strongest evidence, and Darwin designs a 250nt dsRNA molecule with 100% specificity to silence it.

From model organism to crop target to synthesizable RNA construct. Five steps, one workspace.

And Darwin always labels what's measured vs. what's inferred — because a prediction presented as fact is worse than no prediction at all.
